# Saturday Visitation Partner Guide

This guide provides practical instructions for church soulwinners, bus captains, and visitation partnerships conducting Saturday door-to-door outreach and route calling.

---

## 🤝 Partnership Dynamics & Preparation

### 1. The Power of Two (Mark 6:7)
- Always go out in partnerships of two (Senior Partner & Junior Partner).
- **Lead Partner**: Leads the conversation, presents the Gospel, and takes notes.
- **Silent / Prayer Partner**: Prays silently, watches for external safety hazards (traffic, dogs), and assists with holding flyers or tracts.

### 2. Digital Preparation in RouteApp (9:30 AM)
1. Open **My Visits (`/my-visits`)** or **Active Map (`/soulwinning`)**.
2. If assigned specific bus households: review last week's notes and rider names.
3. If knocking an unassigned territory: open **Map Assignments (`/maps/assignments`)** and check out an available neighborhood grid cell.
4. Verify your phone GPS is enabled so RouteApp can auto-detect door numbers.

---

## 🚪 At the Door: Step-by-Step Etiquette

### 1. The Approach
- Walk up the main driveway or walkway; never cut across manicured lawns.
- Stand 4–6 feet back from the door so the resident has comfortable breathing room when opening.
- Smile warmly and maintain friendly eye contact.

### 2. Door Knocking Etiquette
- Knock firmly (3 solid knocks) or ring the doorbell once.
- Wait patiently for at least 30–45 seconds.
- If no one answers:
  - Place a door-hanger invitation or gospel tract securely under the doorknob or behind the storm door handle (never in the US Mailbox, as this violates federal postal laws).
  - Open `/my-visits` or `/soulwinning` ➔ Tap **Not Home (Left Flyer)**.

### 3. When Someone Answers
```
"Good morning! My name is [Name], and this is my friend [Partner Name] from [Church Name] right here in town. 
We're out in the neighborhood inviting families to church this Sunday, and we wanted to stop by and leave you with a quick invitation!"
```

- **If they have children/teens**: Introduce the free church bus ministry!
  - *"We run a free bus throughout this neighborhood every Sunday morning that picks up kids for Sunday School, exciting games, and Bible lessons. Do you have any kids or grandkids who might enjoy riding?"*
- **If they express interest**:
  - Tap **+ Add Prospect / Rider** in RouteApp.
  - Fill in child names, ages, and guardian cell phone.
  - Send the digital permission slip link via SMS right on their doorstep!

### 4. Presenting the Gospel
If the resident is open:
- Transition smoothly: *"We love helping families, but the most important thing our church shares is how you can know for 100% certainty that you're on your way to Heaven when you die. May I take just two minutes to show you what the Bible says?"*
- Walk through the Roman's Road or your church's Gospel plan.
- If they pray to receive Christ:
  - Celebrate and rejoice with them!
  - Record the decision under **Spiritual Decision (Salvation)** in RouteApp.
  - Schedule a follow-up discipleship visit for the following week.

---

## 🚫 Safety & Sensitive Situations

- **Beware of Dogs**: Never enter a yard if a loose dog is barking or approaching. Mark the household note: *"Beware of Dog"*.
- **"No Soliciting" Signs**: Respect signs and move to the next home unless your church has a specific legal canvassing exemption in that municipality.
- **Hostile / Angry Residents**: Never argue. Politely say *"Thank you for your time, have a wonderful weekend!"* and mark the record as **Refused / Do Not Return** in RouteApp so future teams do not disturb the home.
