# Drivers List & Run Sheets (`/drivers-list`)

The **Drivers List** page (`/drivers-list`) is purpose-built for bus drivers, substitute drivers, and dispatchers. It delivers high-contrast, distraction-minimized run sheets optimized for dashboard tablet mounts and mobile steering wheel clips.

---

## 👥 How Different Roles Use This Page

| Role | What You See & Do on This Page |
|---|---|
| **Bus Driver** | Your main in-vehicle cockpit during the route. View large-text stop addresses, see how many kids to expect at each curb, read driver turn notes, and tap **🧭 Navigate** for 1-touch GPS directions. |
| **Substitute / Guest Driver** | View the entire route sequence cleanly without needing to navigate complex menus. Print out or follow the digital step-by-step turn sheet. |
| **Bus Captain (Front Passenger Seat)** | Sit beside the driver with this view open to anticipate upcoming turns, call waiting guardians before arrival, and confirm curb addresses. |
| **Sunday Dispatcher / Fleet Director** | Ensure drivers have loaded their digital run sheets and print backup PDF run sheets for all routes before 7:30 AM. |

## 🛠️ Step-by-Step Actions: What to Do

### 1. Launching Navigation (Driver)
1. When pulling away from the depot or completing Stop #1, look at the next stop card.
2. Tap the large green **🧭 NAVIGATE** button.
3. Your device opens **Apple Maps**, **Google Maps**, or **Waze** with the destination coordinates pre-loaded.
4. Voice guidance directs you directly to the passenger loading zone.

### 2. Calling a Waiting Household (Driver / Captain)
1. If no children are outside waiting at the curb:
2. Tap **📞 QUICK CALL GUARDIAN**.
3. The call connects through the church's virtual Route Phone system so your personal mobile number remains hidden.
4. Say: *"Good morning! This is the church bus pulling up outside for Tommy and Sarah!"*

### 3. Printing a Paper Backup Run Sheet (Admin / Dispatcher)
1. Tap **📄 Print PDF Run Sheet** in the top-right corner.
2. RouteApp generates a clean, high-contrast 2-page printout with all stops, turn notes, emergency phone numbers, and child names.
3. Place the printed sheet in the bus binder for substitute driver emergency backup.
