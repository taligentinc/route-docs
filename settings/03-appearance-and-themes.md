# Appearance, Themes & Accessibility

The **Appearance Settings** page (`/settings/appearance`) allows users to customize the visual theme, contrast level, and map presentation styles.

---

## 🌓 Theme Modes

RouteApp provides three primary appearance modes:
1. **System Default**: Automatically matches your operating system preference (switches from light to dark at sunset).
2. **Light Mode**: Clean, crisp white and slate color scheme optimized for outdoor daylight use.
3. **Dark Mode**: Sleek obsidian and midnight slate theme designed to preserve night vision during evening bus runs and conserve battery on OLED mobile screens.

---

## 🗺️ Map Display Presets

Choose your preferred map styling across territory and route views:
- **Streets (Standard)**: High-contrast street names, building footprints, and highway shields.
- **Satellite / Hybrid**: High-resolution aerial imagery overlay with road labels.
- **Dark Navigation**: Vector dark style reducing glare during night driving.
- **Topographic / Outdoors**: Elevation contours for rural church routes.

---

## ♿ Accessibility & High Contrast

- **Enlarged Touch Targets**: Increases button and tap sizes on driver run sheets.
- **High-Contrast Text**: Forces pure black/white contrast ratios meeting WCAG AAA guidelines.
- **Reduced Motion**: Disables non-essential UI animations for improved responsiveness on older hardware.
