# Authentication, 2FA & Passkeys (WebAuthn)

RouteApp implements enterprise-grade authentication designed to keep ministry data, child contact records, and fleet records secure while offering frictionless access for field workers on mobile devices.

---

## 🔑 Passkeys (WebAuthn / FIDO2)

Passkeys replace traditional passwords with cryptographic public-key credentials tied directly to your device's biometric sensors (Apple Face ID, Touch ID, Android Biometric Prompt, Windows Hello, or YubiKey).

### Why Use Passkeys?
- **Phishing Resistant**: Passkeys cannot be intercepted or spoofed.
- **Fast Login**: Sign in with a fingerprint or glance at your phone in under 2 seconds.
- **No Password Fatigue**: Never forget complex passwords on Sunday mornings.

### Enrolling a Passkey
1. Navigate to **Settings** > **Security** (`/settings/security`).
2. Under the **Passkeys & Biometrics** section, click **+ Register New Passkey**.
3. Your browser or mobile operating system will prompt you to authenticate via Touch ID, Face ID, or your device PIN.
4. Name your passkey (e.g., *"Pastor's iPhone 15"* or *"Bus Tablet 2"*).
5. The passkey is now registered and active!

### Well-Known Passkey Endpoints
For automated password managers (1Password, Bitwarden, Apple Keychain), RouteApp provides standard discovery endpoints:
- `/.well-known/passkey-endpoints`

## 📱 Two-Factor Authentication (2FA / TOTP)

For accounts handling administrative permissions, two-factor authentication adds a required secondary verification code.

### Enabling TOTP Two-Factor Authentication
1. Go to **Settings** > **Security** (`/settings/security`).
2. Click **Enable Two-Factor Authentication**.
3. Scan the generated QR code using your preferred authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, 1Password, Authy).
4. Enter the 6-digit confirmation code generated by your app.
5. **Important**: Save your emergency **Recovery Codes** in a safe place. If you ever lose your phone, recovery codes are the only way to regain access without administrator intervention.

## 🌐 Single Sign-On (SSO) & Social Logins

Churches utilizing Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, or Apple IDs can sign in seamlessly via OAuth 2.0 / OpenID Connect:
- **Google Sign-In**: Ideal for ministries using Google Workspace.
- **Microsoft / Azure AD**: Integrates with church organizational Microsoft accounts.
- **Apple Sign-In**: Quick authentication for iOS and macOS users.

> **Note for Administrators**: SSO accounts must have an email matching an approved worker profile within the church tenant.
