# Active Territory Map (`/maps` & `/soulwinning`)

The **Active Territory Map** (`/maps` or `/soulwinning`) is an interactive, GPU-accelerated Mapbox GL mapping environment designed for church soulwinning teams, bus captains, outreach coordinators, and pastors to visualize church territories, neighborhood blocks, route boundaries, and field workers.

---

## 👥 How Different Roles Use This Page

| Role | What You See & Do on This Page |
|---|---|
| **Soulwinner / Canvasser** | View available neighborhood grid cells, tap to check out a territory block, use GPS centering while walking streets, log doors and decisions, and submit bus prospects. |
| **Bus Captain** | See all your bus stops as color-coded pins, inspect route boundary geofences, and find unreached streets within your route territory. |
| **Outreach Director / Pastor** | View master church coverage, inspect which neighborhood blocks have been knocked in the last 30/60/90 days, and assign territory blocks to Bible college or Sunday School teams. |
| **Fleet Dispatcher** | View real-time GPS locations of all church buses and field workers on Sunday mornings. |

## 🛠️ Step-by-Step Actions: What to Do

### A. How to Center on Your Current GPS Location (Soulwinner / Driver)
1. When standing on a neighborhood sidewalk or driving a bus:
2. Tap the blue **📍 My Location** floating button.
3. The map zooms directly to your position with a pulsing blue GPS beacon.
4. As you walk down the street, your beacon moves in real time.

### B. How to Check Out a Territory Block (Soulwinner)
1. Tap on any **Gray (Available)** grid cell on the map.
2. A preview card opens showing:
   - Cell ID (e.g. *Cell #104: Elm St to 14th St*)
   - Approximate homes: *~120 houses*
   - Last visited: *72 days ago*
3. Tap **Check Out This Territory**.
4. Select your partner from the active workers list.
5. The cell turns **Yellow (Checked Out)** across all church maps.

### C. How to Log a Door While Canvassing (Soulwinner / Partner)
1. Knock on a door.
2. Tap **+ LOG DOOR / CONTACT**.
3. RouteApp detects your street address from your phone's GPS automatically.
4. Select the outcome:
   - 🚪 **Not Home (Left Flyer)**
   - 🗣️ **Spoke with Resident (Gave Invite)**
   - 📖 **Gospel Presentation Given**
   - ✝️ **Salvation / Decision Recorded**
   - 🚌 **Interested in Bus Ministry (Add Rider Prospect)**
5. Tap **Save Contact**. The house marker updates on your map instantly.
