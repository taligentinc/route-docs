# Soulwinning CRM & Canvassing

The **Soulwinning CRM** (`/soulwinning` / `SoulwinningList`) transforms door-to-door evangelism into an organized, follow-up-driven ministry.

---

## 🚪 Real-Time Canvassing Logging

While walking a neighborhood block:
1. Tap **+ Log Door / Contact**.
2. RouteApp captures your current GPS address automatically (or enter address manually).
3. Select the **Interaction Outcome**:
   - 🚪 **Not Home**: Left door-hanger / gospel tract.
   - 🗣️ **Spoke with Resident**: Had a brief conversation; gave invitation.
   - 📖 **Gospel Presentation Given**: Full plan of salvation shared.
   - ✝️ **Salvation / Spiritual Decision**: Resident accepted Christ, requested baptism, or rededicated life.
   - 🚌 **Bus Ride Prospect**: Children/teens interested in riding the bus.
   - 🚫 **Refused / Do Not Return**: Resident requested no further visits.

---

## 🌟 Converting Prospects to Bus Riders

When a parent says *"Yes, I would love my kids to ride the Sunday bus"*:
1. Check the box **"Interested in Bus Ministry"**.
2. Enter children's names and grade levels.
3. Tap **Create Prospect & Notify Captain**.
4. RouteApp automatically:
   - Pins the location on the corresponding bus route map.
   - Sends a push notification / SMS alert to the route captain.
   - Queues the family for Saturday morning bus visitation.

---

## ✝️ Decisions & Discipleship Tracking

Every recorded salvation is added to the church's discipleship pipeline:
- **Follow-Up Visits**: Assign discipleship materials, beginner Bible study guides, and baptism consultation.
- **Prayer Wall**: Submit praises and prayer requests to the church prayer chain.
- **Reporting**: Weekly soulwinning reports generated automatically for Sunday bulletin and pastoral review.
