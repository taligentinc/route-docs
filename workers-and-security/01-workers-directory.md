# Workers Directory & Staff Management

The **Workers Directory** (`/workers`) is the administrative hub for managing all church staff, bus captains, drivers, mechanics, soulwinners, and ministry volunteers.

---

## 👥 Adding & Inviting New Workers

1. Navigate to `/workers` and click **+ Add Worker**.
2. **Worker Information**:
   - First & Last Name
   - Email Address (used for login and password resets)
   - Mobile Phone Number
   - Role (Admin, Captain, Driver, Mechanic, Worker, Soulwinner, Viewer)
   - Assigned Primary Route & District
3. **Account Creation Options**:
   - **Send Invitation Email**: Generates an onboarding email with a temporary password and direct login link.
   - **Manual Password Set**: Administrator manually sets a temporary password and provides it to the worker.

---

## 🔒 Managing Roles & Route Permissions

In the worker detail view:
- **Change Roles**: Elevate a worker to Captain or assign Driver privileges.
- **Multi-Route Access**: Check multiple routes if a worker covers Route 1 on Sundays and assists on Route 4 on Wednesdays.
- **Deactivate / Suspend Account**: If a volunteer moves away or steps down, deactivate their login immediately while preserving historical logs and attribution.

---

## 🛡️ Background Checks & Safety Certifications

For child safety compliance:
- Track **Background Check Date & Expiration**
- Track **Child Protection Training Certification**
- Track **Driver Commercial Driver's License (CDL) & Medical Card Expiration**
- Color-coded badges alert administrators 30 days before certifications expire.
