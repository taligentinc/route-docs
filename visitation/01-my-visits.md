# My Visits (Personal Visitation Log)

The **My Visits** page (`/my-visits`) gives every worker a personalized, focused dashboard to view their assigned households, record Saturday visitation outcomes, note spiritual decisions, and submit prayer requests.

---

## 📋 The Personal Visitation View

When logged in as a worker or captain:
- Displays all households, riders, or prospects currently assigned to you for visitation this week.
- Sorted by distance or stop order.
- Quick indicators show when each household was last visited.

---

## ✍️ Logging a Visit Outcome

To log a visit:
1. Tap on the household card or click **+ Log Visit**.
2. Select the **Visit Result**:
   - 🟢 **Home & Coming Sunday**: Confirmed attendance for the upcoming service.
   - 🟡 **Home - Not Coming**: Home, spoke with guardian, but cannot attend this week (e.g. sick, visiting family).
   - ⚪ **Not Home / Left Flyer**: Knocked on door, left door-hanger or tract.
   - 🔴 **Moved / Inactive**: Family has relocated; prompts for address update or archiving.
   - ⭐ **Spiritual Decision**: Salvation, baptism interest, church dedication, or prayer request.
3. **Notes & Prayer Requests**: Enter notes regarding needs, prayer requests, or family updates.
4. **Follow-Up Date**: Set a reminder date if follow-up is needed before Sunday.
5. Tap **Save Visit**.

---

## 📈 Personal Visitation Statistics

At the top of `/my-visits`, track your ministry activity:
- **Visits Completed This Week**
- **Families Reached**
