# Rider & Prospect Management

RouteApp distinguishes between active **Riders** (regularly enrolled passengers) and **Prospects** (potential riders or soulwinning contacts needing follow-up).

---

## 📇 Prospects Directory (`/prospects`)

The **Prospects List** (`/prospects`) contains all contacted individuals who have expressed interest in riding the bus or attending church:

### Key Features:
- **Prospect Statuses**: `New Lead`, `Visited - Follow Up`, `Ready to Route`, `Enrolled as Rider`, `Not Interested`.
- **Search & Filter**: Filter by district, bus route, date added, or soulwinning worker.
- **Conversion Workflow**: When a prospect agrees to ride the bus, click **Convert to Active Rider**. RouteApp will prompt you to link them to an existing stop or create a new stop on their assigned route.

---

## 🧒 Rider Profiles & Fields

Every rider record contains essential ministry information:

| Field | Description | Importance |
|---|---|---|
| **Full Name** | First and Last name | Used on Sunday school rosters and roll call |
| **Date of Birth / Age** | Child age calculation | Auto-determines grade and class division |
| **Grade Level** | Toddler, Pre-K, K–12, College, Adult | Grouping for Sunday School class distribution |
| **Gender** | Male / Female | Class placement and seating |
| **Medical / Allergies** | Peanut allergy, Asthma, Diabetes, etc. | Prominently badged in red on My Route for safety |
| **Special Needs** | Wheelchair access, sensory needs | Alerts driver for lift equipment and helper assignment |
| **Sunday School Class** | Beginner, Primary, Junior, Teens | Printed on the Sunday morning manifest |
| **Permission Slip** | Linked digital or scanned waiver | Legal compliance and liability protection |

---

## 📝 Grade Selection Field Customization

RouteApp supports standard and customized school grade tiers:
- **Nursery / Toddler**: (Ages 0–3)
- **Pre-K & Kindergarten**: (Ages 4–5)
- **Elementary (1st – 5th Grade)**: Primary & Junior boys/girls
- **Middle School (6th – 8th Grade)**: Junior High
- **High School (9th – 12th Grade)**: Teen Ministry
- **Adult / Senior**: Adult Bible class riders

---

## 🚨 Emergency Contacts & Safety Flags

To protect children and support drivers during unexpected situations:
- Each rider record can hold multiple verified emergency phone numbers.
- **Custody / Pickup Restrictions**: If a parent requests that specific individuals are **not** permitted to pick up or receive the child from the bus stop, a prominent **Warning Banner** appears on the stop card and driver run sheet.
