# Profile & Notification Settings

The **Profile Settings** page (`/settings/profile`) allows users to manage their personal account information, preferred navigation apps, default routes, and notification channels.

---

## 👤 Personal Information
- **Full Name**: Displayed on audit logs, manifests, and chat rooms.
- **Email Address**: Used for login and administrative correspondence.
- **Phone Number**: Used for SMS verification and softphone identity.
- **Default Bus Route**: Choose your primary route to load automatically upon login.

---

## 🔔 Notification Preferences

Configure how and when RouteApp alerts you:
- **Incoming Call Ringers**: In-app sound and push banner when a parent calls your route number.
- **New SMS Messages**: Instant notification when a parent texts your route.
- **Registration Alerts**: Be notified when a new public ride registration is submitted for your route.
- **Fleet & DVIR Alerts**: Instant notification when a driver reports a critical vehicle defect.
- **Notification Channels**: Enable/Disable Browser Web Push, Mobile APNs Push, or Email Digests.
