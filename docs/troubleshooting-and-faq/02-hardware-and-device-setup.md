# Hardware & In-Vehicle Tablet Setup Guide

This guide details recommended hardware, dashboard mounting solutions, power management, and tablet setups for church buses and ministry vans.

---

## 📱 Recommended Devices

| Device Tier | Recommended Models | Pros / Best Use Case |
|---|---|---|
| **Dedicated Bus Tablet** | Apple iPad (9th/10th Gen 10.2") or Samsung Galaxy Tab A9+ | Large screen for driver turn-by-turn navigation & attendance roll call |
| **Rugged Field Tablet** | Samsung Galaxy Tab Active4 Pro | Drop-resistant, water-resistant, operable with work gloves |
| **Personal Smartphone** | iPhone 12+ / Android 10+ | Captains and soulwinners conducting door-to-door visitation |

---

## 🚗 In-Vehicle Dashboard Mounting

For safety and DOT compliance, tablets must be securely mounted and within the driver's natural line of sight without obstructing windshield view:

1. **Heavy-Duty Suction / Cup Holder Mounts**: (RAM Mounts or Arkon Heavy-Duty).
2. **Steering Column / Doghouse Bolt-Down Mounts**: Preferred for full-size school buses (Thomas, Blue Bird, IC Bus).
3. **Safety Rule**: Mount the tablet so the driver does not have to reach or look down while operating the vehicle.

---

## ⚡ Power Management & Thermal Protection

- **Continuous 12V Power**: Use heavy-duty 2.4A+ USB-C vehicle chargers connected to an ignition-switched 12V accessory port.
- **Sun & Thermal Throttling**: Direct sunlight on dashboards can cause tablets to overheat. Use sunshades or mount the tablet lower on the doghouse dash away from direct windshield glare.
- **Screen Auto-Lock**: Set tablet screen timeout to "Never" while plugged into power so the driver run sheet remains visible throughout the route.
