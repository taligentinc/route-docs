# Driver Vehicle Inspection Report (DVIR) Wizard (`/vehicles/dvir`)

The **DVIR Wizard** (`/vehicles/dvir`) is RouteApp's digital safety inspection system. It enforces DOT/FMCSA compliance, protects children, prevents roadside breakdowns, and ensures continuous communication between drivers and mechanics.

---

## 👥 How Different Roles Use This Page

| Role | What You See & Do on This Page |
|---|---|
| **Bus Driver / Van Operator** | Complete your mandatory Pre-Trip walkaround before turning on the ignition, log any defects with camera photos, and submit your Post-Trip Child Safety Sweep upon return. |
| **Fleet Mechanic** | Review incoming defect reports generated by drivers in real time, see attached photos of worn parts or leaks, and verify driver pre-trip signatures. |
| **Transportation Director / Admin** | Monitor which church vehicles have completed their daily pre-trip inspections and verify that zero vehicles leave the bus barn with unresolved critical defects. |

## 🛠️ Step-by-Step Actions: What to Do

### A. Completing a Pre-Trip Inspection (Driver)
1. Arrive at the bus depot at least 20–30 minutes before route departure.
2. Open `/vehicles/dvir` on your phone or bus tablet.
3. Select your assigned vehicle and choose **Pre-Trip Inspection**.
4. Enter starting mileage and current fuel level.
5. Physically perform the 4-step walkaround, checking off each item as you inspect it.
6. If everything passes, sign your name on the digital pad and tap **🟢 Submit Pre-Trip Report**.
7. The vehicle status turns green: **Ready for Service**.

### B. Reporting a Defect (Driver)
1. If you discover a problem (e.g. low tire tread or a cracked clearance light lens):
2. Tap **+ Report Defect** next to that component.
3. Select Severity:
   - 🔴 **Critical (Out of Service)**: Braking defect, bald tire, stop-arm malfunction, fuel leak. Immediately locks the vehicle from being driven until a mechanic signs off.
   - 🟡 **Minor (Needs Maintenance)**: Dome light out, cracked mirror casing, wiper streak. Flagged for mechanics, but the vehicle remains safe to operate today.
4. Tap **Take Photo** to snap a clear picture of the issue.
5. Type a brief description (e.g., *"Right rear outer tire has a nail in tread, holding 75 PSI"*).
6. Tap **Attach Defect**.

### C. Completing the Post-Trip Child Safety Sweep (Driver & Captain)
1. Upon returning to the depot and parking the vehicle:
2. Walk from the driver seat all the way to the back row of the bus, looking on and under every seat.
3. Open `/vehicles/dvir` and select **Post-Trip Inspection**.
4. Check the mandatory legal declaration: *"I certify that I have physically walked to the rear of the bus and inspected every row. No children or personal items remain onboard."*
5. Sign your name and tap **Complete Post-Trip**.
