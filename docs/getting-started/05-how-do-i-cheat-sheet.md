# ⚡ "How Do I...?" Quick Reference Cheat Sheet

Welcome to the **RouteApp Quick Reference Cheat Sheet**! If you need to do something quickly on Sunday morning or Saturday visitation, find your task below for simple 1-2-3 instructions.

---

## 🚌 Sunday Morning Bus Operations

### How do I check in a child when they get on the bus?
1. Open **Attendance (`/attendance`)** or **My Route (`/my-route`)**.
2. Find the child's name under their stop.
3. **Tap the child's name badge once** ➔ It turns green 🟢 **Present (Boarded)**.
4. If they aren't riding today, tap their badge to turn it gray ⚪ **Absent**.

---

### How do I add a new kid (walk-on visitor) who isn't on my list?
1. Open **Attendance (`/attendance`)**.
2. Tap the blue **+ Add Walk-On Visitor** button at the top.
3. Type the child's name, age/grade, and their parent's phone number.
4. Tap **Add to Bus**. The child is added to your bus count immediately, and their parent gets an automatic text to sign a permission slip!

---

### How do I see directions to the next house?
1. On **My Route (`/my-route`)** or **Drivers List (`/drivers-list`)**, find the stop.
2. Tap the large **🧭 Navigate** button.
3. Your phone opens Apple Maps, Google Maps, or Waze and directs you straight to the curb!

---

### How do I call or text a parent?
- **To Text**: Tap the **💬 SMS** icon on the stop card to open the chat thread. Your message sends from the church's official Route Phone line, keeping your personal number private.
- **To Call**: Tap the **📞 Call** icon on the stop card to dial through the in-app softphone.

---

### How do I switch between different bus routes?
1. Look at the top bar on any screen where it shows your current route name (e.g., `[ 🚌 Route 3 - Eastside ] ▾`).
2. Tap that route name.
3. Pick any route you want to view from the list. The entire app switches to that route instantly.

---

## 🚗 Vehicle Safety & Inspections

### How do I do the morning pre-trip inspection before driving?
1. Open **Vehicles** > **DVIR (`/vehicles/dvir`)**.
2. Select your bus number and tap **Pre-Trip Inspection**.
3. Type starting mileage and current fuel level.
4. Walk around your bus and check off the items (oil, tires, lights, stop-arm, emergency exits, brakes).
5. Sign your name at the bottom and tap **🟢 Submit Pre-Trip Report**.

---

### How do I report a broken part or flat tire?
1. In the DVIR inspection, tap **+ Report Defect** next to the broken item.
2. Pick **Critical** (unsafe to drive) or **Minor** (needs repair later).
3. Take a photo with your phone camera and type a quick note.
4. Tap **Attach Defect**. The church mechanic will see it on their screen instantly!

---

### How do I do the post-trip child safety sweep after the route?
1. Park the bus at the church depot.
2. Turn off the engine and **physically walk to the very last row of the bus**, checking on top of and under every single seat.
3. Open `/vehicles/dvir` ➔ select **Post-Trip Inspection**.
4. Check the box confirming no sleeping children remain onboard, sign your name, and tap **Complete Post-Trip**.

---

## 🚪 Saturday Visitation & Outreach

### How do I mark who is riding tomorrow during Saturday visits?
1. Open **My Visits (`/my-visits`)** on Saturday morning.
2. Tap on the family you are visiting:
   - 🟢 **Coming Sunday**: They confirmed they will ride tomorrow!
   - 🟡 **Not Coming**: Home, but sick or out of town.
   - ⚪ **Not Home**: Left a door flyer.
   - ⭐ **Spiritual Decision**: Someone got saved or made a decision for Christ!
3. Tap **Save Visit**.

---

### How do I add a new family or house we met while out?
1. Open **My Route (`/my-route`)** and tap **+ Add Stop**.
2. Type the street address (RouteApp auto-completes city/state/zip).
3. Type the parent's name and mobile phone number.
4. Tap **Save Stop**.
5. On the new stop card, tap **+ Add Rider** to add each child's name and grade.

---

### How do I send a permission slip link to a parent's phone?
1. Open the Stop or Rider detail screen.
2. Tap **📱 Send E-Sign Link via SMS**.
3. The parent receives a text message with a secure link (`/ride/sign/{id}`).
4. The parent signs with their finger on their smartphone screen in 10 seconds!
