# Welcome to RouteApp: Simple Overview

Welcome to **RouteApp** (`app.routeapp.org`)! 

RouteApp is designed to make running a church bus route, soulwinning outreach, and church vehicle fleet as simple, safe, and stress-free as possible. It replaces paper clipboards, lost index cards, binder checklists, and confusing text chains with a single easy app on your phone, tablet, or computer.

---

## 🌟 What Can You Do in RouteApp?

### 1. 🚌 Run Your Sunday Morning Bus Route
- See all your stops in order with clear street addresses and 1-tap driving directions.
- See every child's name, grade level, parent phone number, and important allergy alerts.
- Check kids in as they hop on the bus with a single tap of your finger.
- Add walk-on visitors in 5 seconds.

### 2. 💬 Text & Call Parents with Privacy
- Text or call parents directly from your route's official church phone number.
- Your personal cell phone number is never exposed to anyone.
- Send a quick text to all 30 families on your route at once when buses are running behind schedule.

### 3. 🚗 Keep Vehicles Safe & Inspected (DVIR)
- Easy 4-step walkaround vehicle checklist before you turn on the bus ignition.
- Snap photos of flat tires or broken lights right into the app so mechanics see them instantly.
- Mandatory post-trip child safety sweep to guarantee no sleeping child is left behind.

### 4. 🚪 Knock Doors & Saturday Visitation
- See exactly which families to visit on Saturday morning.
- Mark who is riding tomorrow in one tap (`Coming Sunday`, `Not Coming`, `Left Flyer`).
- Turn new neighborhood families into bus riders with digital permission slips they can sign on their phone in 10 seconds.

---

## 📱 How to Get RouteApp on Your Phone

You don't need to search through an app store:
1. Open your phone browser (**Safari** on iPhone or **Chrome** on Android).
2. Go to your church's web link (e.g. `https://faithbaptist.routeapp.org` or `https://app.routeapp.org/install`).
3. Tap **Share** (on iPhone) or the **Menu Dots** (on Android) and tap **"Add to Home Screen"**.
4. The RouteApp icon appears on your home screen just like any normal app!
