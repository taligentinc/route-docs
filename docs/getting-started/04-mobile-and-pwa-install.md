# Mobile App Installation & Device Permissions

RouteApp is engineered as a **Progressive Web App (PWA)** and native hybrid application, providing a lightning-fast native mobile experience across iPhones, iPads, Android phones, and rugged field tablets without requiring app store updates.

---

## 📲 Installing on iOS (iPhone & iPad)

To install RouteApp on your Apple device:
1. Open **Safari** on your iPhone or iPad.
2. Navigate to your church URL (e.g., `https://faithbaptist.routeapp.org` or `https://app.routeapp.org/install`).
3. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow pointing upward at the bottom of the screen).
4. Scroll down the share sheet and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
5. Confirm the name *"RouteApp"* and tap **Add** in the top right corner.
6. The RouteApp icon will now appear on your home screen. Open it to run full-screen without browser URL bars.

---

## 🤖 Installing on Android

1. Open **Google Chrome** on your Android device.
2. Navigate to your church's RouteApp URL.
3. Chrome will display a banner at the bottom: **"Add RouteApp to Home screen"** (or tap the three-dots menu in the top right and select **Install App**).
4. Tap **Install**.
5. RouteApp will install onto your device like any native Google Play Store application.

---

## 🔒 Required Device Permissions

To unlock all field capabilities, the app may request specific hardware permissions:

| Permission | Purpose in RouteApp | How to Enable |
|---|---|---|
| **Location Services (GPS)** | Real-time map navigation, stop proximity detection, soulwinning cell location tracking, and fleet live monitoring. | Tap **"Allow While Using App"** or **"Always Allow"** when prompted. |
| **Microphone / Audio** | In-app WebRTC softphone for placing and receiving bus phone calls directly inside the app. | Tap **"Allow"** when prompted during first call. |
| **Push Notifications** | Incoming call ringers, new SMS alerts, chat messages, and urgent fleet/route broadcast notices. | Tap **"Allow"** in the browser permission prompt. |
| **Camera** | Snapping photos of DVIR vehicle defects, scanning paper permission slips, and attaching receipts. | Tap **"Allow"** when uploading a photo. |

---

## 🔄 Offline Data Support

RouteApp caches essential route stop lists, phone directories, and territory boundaries locally. If cellular service drops in a rural neighborhood:
- You can continue viewing stops and rider rosters.
- Attendance marks and visit notes are queued locally and automatically sync back to the cloud as soon as connection is restored.
