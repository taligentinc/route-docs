# Chats & Two-Way SMS Messaging (`/chats`)

The **Chats Room** (`/chats`) is a unified communication center connecting church bus captains, drivers, workers, and neighborhood parents in real time. It combines direct two-way parent SMS texting, internal worker group channels, and route broadcast announcements.

---

## 👥 How Different Roles Use This Page

| Role | What You See & Do on This Page |
|---|---|
| **Bus Captain & Co-Captain** | Your primary communication hub. Text parents to confirm Sunday pickup times, answer questions about service schedules, and receive instant alerts when a parent replies. |
| **Bus Driver** | Read urgent traffic or route updates sent by dispatch, or review notes sent by captains regarding specific rider pickup locations. |
| **Bus Worker / Helper** | Participate in internal route channels (e.g. `#route-3-team`) to coordinate who is bringing bus snacks, game prizes, or visiting specific streets. |
| **Parent / Guardian** | Communicates normally from their personal smartphone via SMS without needing to download any app or create a password. |
| **Church Admin / Bus Director** | Broadcast church-wide announcements across all bus routes simultaneously (e.g. weather delays or Friend Day promotions). |

## 🛠️ Step-by-Step Actions: What to Do

### A. How to Send a 2-Way Text to a Parent (Captain / Worker)
1. Open `/chats` on your phone, tablet, or laptop.
2. Click on the parent's name in the left column.
3. Notice that the top header shows the parent's address, stop number, and children's names for quick reference.
4. Type your message in the composer box.
5. Tap **📤 SEND**.
6. The message sends directly to the parent's mobile phone via SMS from your church's official Route Phone number.

### B. How to Send a Route-Wide Broadcast Announcement (Captain / Admin)
1. In the left sidebar, tap **📢 Broadcast Announcement**.
2. Select target audience: **All Active Families on Route 3**.
3. Type your text:
   > *"Good morning Faith Baptist families! Just a reminder that our bus is running about 10 minutes behind schedule today due to snow. See you soon!"*
4. Tap **Send Broadcast**.
5. RouteApp sends individual SMS messages to all 28 families simultaneously.
6. When a parent replies, their response opens in their own private 1-on-1 thread so group replies do not spam other parents.

### C. Using Canned Message Templates (Quick Responses)
1. Inside any chat thread, tap the **⚡ Templates** icon.
2. Select a pre-written template:
   - *"Pickup Confirmation for Sunday"*
   - *"Missing Permission Slip Reminder Link"*
   - *"Bus Arriving in 5 Minutes"*
3. The message text is inserted into your composer, personalized with the child's name automatically. Tap **Send**.
