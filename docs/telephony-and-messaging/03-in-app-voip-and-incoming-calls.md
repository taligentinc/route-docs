# In-App VoIP & Incoming Call Handling

RouteApp includes a native, in-browser and in-app **WebRTC VoIP Softphone** that lets church staff place and receive crystal-clear voice calls without downloading extra softphone apps.

---

## 📞 Receiving an Incoming Call (Incoming Call Modal)

When a parent calls your route's virtual phone number:
1. An **Incoming Call Modal** pops up across all open RouteApp sessions on your desktop, laptop, or mobile screen.
2. A distinctive church ringer plays.
3. **Caller ID Lookup**: RouteApp matches the incoming phone number against the church database and displays:
   - *Parent Name* (e.g., *"Martha Smith"*)
   - *Linked Route & Stop* (e.g., *"Stop #3 - Elm St - Route 2"*)
   - *Children Enrolled* (e.g., *"Tommy Smith, Sarah Smith"*)
4. **Controls**:
   - 🟢 **Answer**: Connects the WebRTC audio call directly in your browser or headset.
   - 🔴 **Decline / Send to Voicemail**: Sends caller to your route's custom voicemail greeting.

---

## 🎙️ Outbound Calling via Softphone

To make an outbound call from the church route number:
1. Click the **📞 Call** button on any Stop Card, Rider profile, or within the Chats room.
2. The softphone dialer initializes in the bottom right corner.
3. The call connects using the church's caller ID, keeping your personal number private.
4. **In-Call Controls**:
   - Mute / Unmute Microphone
   - Numeric DTMF Keypad (for navigating automated phone menus)
   - Hold / Transfer
   - End Call

---

## 📼 Voicemails & Call Recordings

- **Voicemail Audio**: If a call is missed, the voicemail is recorded and transcribed directly into the route's chat timeline.
- **Audio Playback**: Tap **Play** inside the chat thread to listen to parent voicemails directly.
