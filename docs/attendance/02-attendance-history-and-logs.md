# Attendance History, Logs & Analytics

RouteApp maintains full historical attendance logs (`/attendance`) across all services, dates, and bus routes.

---

## 📅 Historical Log Browser

In the attendance view:
1. Select the **Service Date** using the date-picker or quick navigation arrows (`← Previous Sunday`, `Next Sunday →`).
2. Filter by **Service Type**: Sunday Morning, Sunday Night, Wednesday Night, or Special Youth Event.
3. Review exact timestamps of when each rider was checked in and which worker logged the attendance.

---

## 📉 Absentee & Retention Tracking

To keep children from falling through the cracks:
- **Consecutive Absence Filter**: View riders absent for 2, 3, or 4+ consecutive weeks.
- **1-Click Outreach List**: Export consecutive absentees into a Saturday visitation priority list.
- **Attendance Consistency Score**: See percentage attendance rates per child over 3, 6, and 12-month periods.

---

## 📊 High-Level Ministry Metrics

Admins and pastors can view aggregated church-wide metrics:
- **Fleet-Wide Headcount Total**: Sum of all bus riders across all routes.
- **Year-Over-Year Growth**: Comparison with previous ministry seasons and high-attendance Sundays (Friend Day, Fall Kickoff, Easter).
- **Route Comparison Charts**: Identify growing vs. struggling routes.
