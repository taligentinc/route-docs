# Visit Assignments & Workload Distribution

The **Visits Distribution** tool (`/assign-visits`) allows bus captains, directors, and visitation coordinators to efficiently distribute households across workers for Saturday morning visitation.

---

## ⚖️ Balanced Workload Distribution

Instead of manually writing names on index cards:
1. Navigate to `/assign-visits`.
2. Select the **Route** or **District**.
3. View the pool of **Unassigned / Available Households** needing visitation.
4. View the list of **Available Workers / Partnerships** on duty today.

---

## ⚡ Distribution Methods

### 1. 1-Click Auto-Distribute (Equal Balance)
- Click **Auto-Distribute Equally**.
- RouteApp calculates geographic clusters and divides households evenly among available workers (e.g., 8 visits per partnership).
- Clusters minimize driving distance so partners stay within compact neighborhood pockets.

### 2. Manual Drag-and-Drop Assignment
- Drag household cards directly onto a worker's column.
- Rebalance cards as workers arrive.

### 3. Geographic Sector Assignment
- Select a specific street quadrant or apartment complex and assign the entire cluster to a designated worker team.

---

## 📲 Instant Mobile Sync

Once distributed:
- Assigned visits instantly populate each worker's **My Visits (`/my-visits`)** screen on their mobile device.
- Workers receive a push notification when their route list is published.
