# Our Visits (Team & Church-Wide Feed)

The **Our Visits** screen (`/our-visits`) provides church leadership, pastors, and bus directors with a live, real-time activity feed of all visitation efforts happening across the entire church.

---

## 📡 Real-Time Visitation Activity Stream

As workers knock on doors across various bus routes and neighborhoods, entries appear live on the timeline:
- **Worker Name & Route**: Shows who conducted the visit.
- **Household & Stop**: Name of family and address.
- **Outcome Badge**: Color-coded indicator (Confirmed, Left Flyer, Salvation, etc.).
- **Prayer Requests & Notes**: Instant visibility into pastoral care opportunities.

---

## 🔍 Filters & Reporting Controls

Admins and captains can filter the church-wide feed:
- **Date Range**: Today, This Week, Last 30 Days, Custom Range.
- **Route / District Scoping**: View visits specifically for Route 1, Route 2, or Soulwinning District A.
- **Worker Filter**: Review visits logged by a specific worker or partnership.
- **Outcome Filter**: Filter specifically for *Spiritual Decisions* to generate baptism and discipleship lists.

---

## 📊 Ministry Trends & Analytics

The header summary provides aggregated metrics:
- **Total Visits Logged**
- **Contact Rate (%)**: Ratio of families home vs. not home
- **Sunday Projection**: Total confirmed riders for the upcoming Sunday bus fleet
