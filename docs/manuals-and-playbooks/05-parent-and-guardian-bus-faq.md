# Parent & Guardian Bus Ministry FAQ

This guide answers common questions asked by parents, guardians, and families whose children ride the church bus.

---

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### 1. Is the bus ministry really 100% free?
**Yes!** Our bus ministry is completely free of charge. It is a service provided by the church community to provide transportation for children, youth, and families to Sunday School and church services.

---

### 2. What time will my child be picked up and dropped off?
- **Morning Pick-Up**: Your Route Captain will contact you via text or call on Saturday to confirm your approximate pickup window (typically between **7:45 AM and 8:45 AM** depending on your stop order).
- **Afternoon Drop-Off**: Buses typically depart the church campus around **12:00 PM** and deliver children directly back to their homes between **12:15 PM and 1:15 PM**.

---

### 3. How do I know who is driving and supervising the bus?
- Every church vehicle is operated by a licensed, thoroughly screened adult driver holding all required state credentials and background clearances.
- In addition to the driver, each bus has a dedicated **Route Captain and trained adult workers** onboard to supervise children, facilitate games, and ensure seating safety.

---

### 4. What happens if my child is sick or cannot ride this Sunday?
Simply reply to your route's confirmation text message or call the **Route Phone Number** at any time. Our captains see parent text messages immediately and will update the bus roster.

---

### 5. Why do I need to sign a digital permission slip?
For your child's safety and medical protection, church policy requires a parent or legal guardian to complete our online permission slip (`/ride/sign/{id}`). This authorizes transportation and ensures we have your emergency contact information and any medical/allergy details on file.

---

### 6. What rules are children expected to follow on the bus?
To ensure every passenger has a safe and enjoyable ride:
1. Stay seated facing forward at all times while the bus is in motion.
2. Keep hands, arms, and objects inside the bus windows.
3. Treat drivers, captains, and fellow riders with kindness and respect.
4. Listen attentively to song leaders and game directors.
