# Central Administration Portal (/administration)

The **Central Administration Portal** (`/administration` / `AdministrativeDashboard`) is the master management hub for RouteApp platform operators and super administrators.

---

## 🏢 Platform Tenant Overview

Super administrators can view and manage all provisioned church tenants:
- **Tenant Directory**: Searchable list of all active churches, ministries, and missionary organizations.
- **Tenant Metrics**: Total routes, vehicles, active stops, registered riders, and SMS volume per church.
- **Tenant Status**: `Active`, `Provisioning`, `Suspended`, or `Archived`.
- **Domain & Subdomain Routing**: Inspect custom CNAMEs and default subdomains (`<tenant>.routeapp.org`).

---

## 🛠️ Tenant Administration Actions

- **Impersonate Workspace**: Seamlessly log into a church tenant in read-only support mode to troubleshoot configuration issues for church directors.
- **Adjust Resource Quotas**: Allocate additional route capacity, telephony lines, or storage limits.
- **Database Backup & Restoration**: Trigger isolated database backups for individual church tenants.
- **Feature Flag Management**: Enable beta features (e.g. experimental AI route optimization, advanced map styling).
